PowerTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection

PowerTracker accesses your health data via Apple HealthKit (including heart rate, running power, active calories, distance, weight, height, age, and gender). This data is used exclusively to calculate heart rate zones, the TRIMP training load index, and to display your performance metrics.

2. Storage and Security

All received data is processed and stored locally on your device using SwiftData and UserDefaults. We do not use external servers or databases. Your personal data is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.

3. HealthKit Permissions

You can change HealthKit data access permissions at any time in the system "Settings" app under "Health" > PowerTracker.

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@superilia7.com.
